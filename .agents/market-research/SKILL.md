# Market Research

Build market context that improves understanding and awareness. Keep external observations, internal signals, and inferences distinguishable so the artifact remains useful after the immediate decision.

## Workflow

1. Given WHAT needs to be explored, define the market boundary.
2. Identify relevant segments, buyers, users, operators, and alternatives, including manual workarounds and doing nothing.
3. Gather only evidence material. Record source, date, evidence type, and confidence for every consequential claim.
4. Separate observations from interpretations.
5. Identify forces that materially constrain the market:
  - technology, 
  - distribution, 
  - pricing, 
  - regulatory,
  - large players, 
  - ecosystem attributes.
6. Identify market participants:
  - supply side,
  - demand side,
  - segments,
  - key actors,
  - decision makers.
7. State implications, disconfirming evidence sought, and any review trigger.
8. Turn critical gaps into a short, prioritized deep-dive agenda. Recommend a scope for each deep dive without conducting it; persona and journey research remain separate work.
