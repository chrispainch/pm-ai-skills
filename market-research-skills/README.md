# Market Research Skills

This collection creates reusable, evidence-backed market context for strategy and product decisions. It is independent of any one initiative: its findings may reaffirm, refine, or challenge product direction as well as inform Wardley mapping, problem framing, solution shaping, launch planning, and post-launch learning.

## Included Skill

### `market-research`

Builds a decision-oriented view of a market: segments, alternatives, trends, constraints, and confidence. It is not a substitute for initiative-specific user research; use `product-discovery` to validate a selected opportunity with users.

## Output

The standard artifact is `product/strategy/market-research/<research-id>/` with a market research brief and evidence ledger. Findings must identify their source, date, type, and confidence so downstream skills can distinguish observation from inference.
