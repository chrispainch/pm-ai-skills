# Initiative Review Checkpoints

Evaluate the evidence needed to support the initiative's claimed status. These are minimum claims checks, not a mandatory waterfall sequence.

| Status | Minimum claim to support | Material gap if absent |
|---|---|---|
| `framed` | Linked product direction and strategic play, user problem, outcome hypothesis, evidence/assumptions, and opportunity boundary | Cannot be compared or prioritized responsibly |
| `selected` | A portfolio decision that names the initiative and explains its priority | Work may be proceeding without an explicit bet |
| `discovered` | Discovery evidence addresses the assumptions most likely to change the decision | Solution work may be built on untested assumptions |
| `shaped` | Bounded solution, exclusions, risks, dependencies, and success measures | Delivery scope is not stable enough to hand off |
| `delivery` | Delivery specification with observable acceptance criteria and material non-functional requirements | Implementation or release quality cannot be verified |
| `launched` | Launch artifact defines audience, rollout, support readiness, instrumentation, and review cadence | Exposure may outpace readiness or learning |
| `closed` | At least one outcome review and a recorded expand/iterate/pause/retire decision | The initiative has no durable learning record |
| `held` / `stopped` | Framing and a clear current decision with a revisit trigger where relevant | Deferred work will be rediscovered without context |

Review flow, UI exploration, and usability validation when the initiative's user experience is materially complex or the manifest references those artifacts. Their absence is not an automatic failure.

At every checkpoint, flag a direction-alignment gap when an initiative cannot explain which product-direction outcome or boundary it advances, tensions, or challenges. A well-formed initiative may still warrant `return to strategy` when its evidence materially challenges direction.
