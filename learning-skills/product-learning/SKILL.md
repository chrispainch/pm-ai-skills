# Product Learning

Assess whether a released increment changed the intended user and organizational outcomes, then make the next decision explicit. Do not mistake activity metrics for evidence of value.

## Inputs

Use the original outcome hypothesis, expected measures, baselines, product data, qualitative feedback, support signals, and relevant external changes.

## Workflow

1. Restate the original outcome hypothesis and attribution assumptions.
2. Compare observed behavior and outcomes with the baseline and expected direction.
3. Separate data, user feedback, operational signals, and interpretation. Name material measurement gaps.
4. Identify learning about the problem, solution, segment, rollout, and strategic assumptions.
5. Decide whether to expand, iterate, pause, retire, research further, or revisit assumptions.
6. State the artifact or decision record that should be updated; do not leave learning only in a retrospective.
