# Product Direction

Create a durable, falsifiable decision frame that the organization can act on. Direction states what the product exists to change and the strategic choices it will make; it must remain open to revision when evidence challenges its assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Establish scope, time horizon, owner, and organizational context.
2. State the product purpose and desired future in user and system terms, not feature terms.
3. Define target users, buyers, and explicit non-targets.
4. Define a small set of strategic outcomes, product principles, boundaries, and material tradeoffs.
5. Identify outcome measures and guardrails without forcing a misleading single metric.
6. Record assumptions, disconfirming signals, and review triggers.
7. For each material risk or uncertainty, list a next action that reduces it. Assign the action to an accountable business function, such as Product, Research, Design, Engineering, Data, Marketing, Sales, Support, Operations, or Leadership; do not assign an individual unless the user supplies one.
8. Produce an `active` direction. Use `draft` only while the user is still resolving a material gap. If the result would remain draft, ask what is missing or invite the user to make it active; do not present a draft direction as an organizationally relevant outcome.

## Output

Read the [output-locations configuration](../../output-locations.yaml) before creating persistent files. Use `outputs.product-direction.primary` and [references/product-direction-template.md](references/product-direction-template.md). The persistent output is an active product direction.
