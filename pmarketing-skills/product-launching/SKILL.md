# Product Launching

Make rollout legible to internal teams and users. Build on the established solution; do not rewrite it.

## Workflow

1. Restate the problem, solution, expected user outcomes, and expected organizational outcomes briefly.
2. Define internal and external audiences.
3. Explain what changed, how it differs from the prior experience, and how to use it.
4. Define rollout dates or phases, entry criteria, and criteria to progress or pause.
5. Identify internal enablement, user documentation, support content, instrumentation, and operational readiness needs.
6. Record launch risks, fallback paths, quality bars, and unresolved blockers.
7. Link success measures to expected outcomes and state measurement gaps.
8. End with the next concrete action and owner.
